I have realized the role and significance of role division in the design of AI systems. This division of labor made me realize that breaking down complex tasks into specialized sub-tasks can significantly improve the output quality. For instance, there is a clear division of labor between the two. The Planner provides detailed output format templates (including time, location, and cost), while the Reviewer has explicit verification standards and mandatory tool invocation instructions. In addition, I also realized that the cooperation with external tools can significantly and fundamentally change the agent's capabilities. With the real-time search capability, it can verify dynamic information such as the museum's opening hours and ticket prices, greatly enhancing the practicality of the entire system.

I encountered a situation where I didn't know how to ensure that reviewers continuously used the internet_search tool. At the beginning, the agent often relies on its internal knowledge rather than searching for the latest information. Then I attempted to emphasize keywords such as "critical" and "must" multiple times in reviewer instructions, and provide specific search query examples, such as "Louvre museum opening hours 2024". Finally, I added a quantitative standard explicitly requiring "5 to 20 searches "at the end of the instruction, which solved the problem.

I set the output format for the Delta List verification format. Each issue consists of five elements: Problem, Evidence (from search), Impact, Suggested Fix, and Reasoning. This structured feedback is more operational than a simple "good/bad" evaluation. Users can clearly see where changes are needed, why they need to make changes, and how to make changes.
In terms of creativity, the output styles of the two agents I set are different. Planner agent persona gives more priority to user experience and feasibility and will use encouraging language. The reviewer agent persona is more analytical and evidence-based, prioritizing data and requiring internet search support for each suggestion.
